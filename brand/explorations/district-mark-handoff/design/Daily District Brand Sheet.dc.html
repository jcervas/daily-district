The district mark

Five unequal districts, interlocking, with one filled: the district you're looking for today. Every cell is a whole unit on a 3×3 lattice, so the mark rasterises cleanly instead of mushing. The wordmark is unchanged — Barlow 800 outlines, as shipped.

01 · Primary lockup
Navy boundaries, red answer cell. Use this everywhere the ground is light.
02 · Variants
Horizontal — site header, letterhead
Stacked — splash, share card
Reversed — dark mode. Answer cell switches to #FF3B57
Knockout — red panels, won state
One-colour — stamps, fax, embossing, single-plate print
Small cut — filled, not outlined. Favicon, app icon, anything 24px and under
03 · Two cuts

The outlined cut carries the mark's meaning in hairlines, and hairlines are the first thing lost to a raster. Below 24px the outline fills in and the five districts merge. The small cut is filled and tinted instead — redrawn, not shrunk.

04 · Construction & clear space
Lattice  3 × 3 units, one unit = 1/3 mark width
Districts  1×2, 2×1, 1×1, 1×2, 2×1
Answer cell  centre, always the filled one
Gutter  0.04 × mark width
Stroke  0.03 × mark width, never below 1px
Clear space  one lattice unit on all four sides
Mark to wordmark  0.75 unit
Cap height  matches the mark's centre district

06 · Don't

Never make the cells equal. Districts aren't a word grid.
Never colour the districts individually.
Never crowd the wordmark. Hold the 0.75-unit gap.
